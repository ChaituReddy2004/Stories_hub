Welcome! This is a common issue. Usually, it happens because Word uses "hidden" formatting that the Stories Hub converter doesn't always like.

Best practice: Use Kindle Create (a free tool from Amazon) to format your Word file before you upload. It will preserve your intent much better. Also, make sure you're using proper "Styles" in Word for your headings rather than just manual bolding/centering.
